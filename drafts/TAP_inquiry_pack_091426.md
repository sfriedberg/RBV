## Working thesis (inquiry + slide 1)

DT2216 is a BCL-XL PROTAC with FDA orphan designation in T-cell lymphoma and Fast Track in relapsed/refractory PTCL and CTCL. Published CTCL and T-ALL xenografts show single-agent activity versus navitoclax with less platelet toxicity. The solid-tumor Phase 1a established a human dose and peripheral BCL-XL degradation but did not show single-agent activity — and did not enroll CTCL. The ask is to fund the first CTCL and/or PTCL clinical cohort under the existing IND, not to rediscover the target.

Do not write CTCL as the live clinical program. The registered company-sponsored combination study is platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (NCT06964009). A TAP reviewer will see that.
