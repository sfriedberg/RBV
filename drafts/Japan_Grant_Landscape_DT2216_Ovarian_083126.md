## The Large Option:

AMED Strengthening Program for Pharmaceutical Startup Ecosystem is the CPRIT Product Development twin. It funds company-run nonclinical, Phase 1, Phase 2, or exploratory clinical development of a drug, including cancer. The subsidized-cost ceiling is about $67 million (10 billion yen; larger proposals are allowed). AMED pays up to two-thirds, or about $44 million. A certified lead venture firm must put in at least one-third of subsidized costs, and at least about $6.7 million when the candidate is already chosen. DT2216 is a chosen candidate.

The applicant is an unlisted Japan-registered company with a Japan research hub. A foreign 100 percent parent plus Japan subsidiary is an explicit path if the certified firm funds the parent and the Japan company receives at least one-third of subsidized costs. Overseas development is allowed once the Japan hub exists.

The program runs about four times a year. The fourteenth call closed 19 August 2026 at noon Japan time. Hearing is 15 October 2026 for applicants only. The thirteenth call closed 15 May and was awarded 27 August; guidelines put start around early November. The fifteenth call is not posted. The last four windows were 15 October to 12 November 2025, 22 January to 20 February 2026, 14 April to 15 May 2026, and 21 July to 19 August 2026. Judgment: the next window is around mid-October to mid-November 2026.

AMED takes confidential consults from companies and venture firms when a call is not open (v-eco@amed.go.jp). That is the workstream now: stand up a Japan company and start a certified-venture conversation before the next call.
