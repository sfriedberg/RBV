## Compliance notes (IRB / advertising-safe)

**Use this checklist before any outreach leaves Dialectic or a partner.**

### What NOT to claim

- Do **not** claim DT2216 or the combination is safe, effective, curative, or “promising results in patients” for ovarian cancer. This is an early-phase dose-finding study.
- Do **not** cite unpublished preclinical regressions (e.g., PDX “complete regression”) in patient-facing advocacy copy.
- Do **not** compare favorably to approved drugs (Elahere, relacorilant, etc.) or imply this replaces standard care.
- Do **not** guarantee eligibility, enrollment, or treatment assignment.
- Do **not** use “breakthrough,” “game-changing,” “hope for a cure,” or similar hype.
- Do **not** ask advocacy groups to give medical advice, interpret eligibility, or screen patients for Dialectic.
- Do **not** offer incentives for enrollment referrals that could look like inducement (unless vetted by counsel/IRB).

### What TO ask advocacy partners to do

- Share the **ClinicalTrials.gov** link and/or DFCI trial page.
- Direct interested patients to **discuss with their oncologist** and/or call the **site contact / DFCI clinical trials line**.
- Optionally list the trial in an existing clinical-trial finder / newsletter / community board using **IRB-approved or publicly registered language** only.
- Clarify that participation is voluntary and that the care team decides appropriateness.

### Suggested disclaimer (append to partner posts if they want a short footer)

> This message is for information only and is not medical advice. Clinical trials have risks and eligibility requirements. Patients should talk with their oncology team. Study details: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06964009.

### Process note

Final external language should be reviewed by Dialectic (medical/legal) and, where required, aligned with site/IRB advertising rules. Advocacy orgs often require sponsor attestation that listed trials are open and accurately described.

**Re:** Sharing an actively recruiting ovarian cancer clinical trial (NCT06964009)

Dear [Name / Team],

I am writing from Dialectic Therapeutics to ask whether your organization would consider sharing accurate information about a clinical trial that is currently recruiting in Boston for people with recurrent ovarian cancer, including platinum-resistant disease.

**What the study is**  
The study (ClinicalTrials.gov **NCT06964009**; Dana-Farber protocol **25-076**) is a **phase 1b** trial of **DT2216**, an investigational BCL-XL degrader (a PROTAC), given with **weekly paclitaxel**. The publicly stated purpose is to find the highest dose of DT2216 that can be given with paclitaxel in a way that is reasonably safe and tolerable in recurrent ovarian cancer. It is **not** a late-phase efficacy trial, and we are careful not to overstate what is known.

**Who leads it / where**  
- Lead principal investigator: **Elizabeth Stover, MD, PhD**, Dana-Farber Cancer Institute  
- Locations listed on ClinicalTrials.gov: **Dana-Farber** and **Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center** are recruiting; **Brigham and Women’s Hospital** is listed as not yet recruiting  
- Patient inquiry line at Dana-Farber: **877-DF-TRIAL (877-338-7425)**  
- Study contact on ClinicalTrials.gov: Dr. Stover — **617-632-6866** / elizabeth_stover@dfci.harvard.edu

We are **not** asking you to recommend the trial, interpret eligibility, or provide medical advice. We would be grateful if you would consider:

1. Posting or circulating the **ClinicalTrials.gov** and/or Dana-Farber trial links through channels you already use for trial education; and/or
