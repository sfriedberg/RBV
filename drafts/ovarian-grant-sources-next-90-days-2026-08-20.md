## Priority narrative (investor-grade)

**Near-term application stack (next ~90 days):**

1. **CDMRP OCRP CTA** is the only ovarian-specific, clinical-trial-scale federal mechanism with a live LOI in September and a hard ceiling (~$2.8M) that can materially underwrite a Ph1b/2. Organizations may be for-profit or nonprofit; independent investigators at all levels may serve as PI. Regulatory readiness (IND/IDE documentation) is baked into the PA — align Dialectic’s IND package with Stover’s clinical narrative before 15 Sep. Sources: [cdmrp.health.mil/funding/ocrp](https://cdmrp.health.mil/funding/ocrp); [HT942526OCRPCTA](https://cdmrp.health.mil/funding/pa/HT942526OCRPCTA_GG.pdf); [eBRAP eligibility excerpt](https://ebrap.org/eBRAP/public/ViewFile.htm?fileId=36039260&fileType=pdf).

2. **Gateway** remains the best philanthropic Phase I/II specialist: exclusive early-phase focus, DFCI precedent (e.g., Gateway-funded DFCI investigators publicly cited), and explicit for-profit applicant eligibility. Cap $1.5M / average ~$500k — treat as **partial trial underwrite or expansion/correlatives**, not sole financing. Sources: [gatewaycr.org FAQs](https://gatewaycr.org/for-researchers/research-faqs); [smapply LOI](https://gatewaycr.smapply.org/prog/gfcr_all_grant_programs).

3. **NCI SBIR/STTR** is the clean **company-direct** path. Sep 5 is immediately adjacent; if bandwidth is consumed by CDMRP, **January 5, 2027** is still inside the stated early-2027 LOI horizon. Pair STTR with DFCI as research institution if IP/collaboration structure fits. Source: [sbir.cancer.gov Omnibus](https://sbir.cancer.gov/small-business-funding/grants/omnibus-solicitation).

4. **Tina’s Wish A2C (LOI 9 Sep)** is the nearest *ovarian-named* philanthropic LOI after SBIR, but the mandate is **biomarker acceleration to clinic** (EDRN framework), not open-ended therapeutic combination funding. Pursue only with a real companion-biomarker aim. Source: [tinaswish.org/acceleration-to-clinic-grant](https://tinaswish.org/acceleration-to-clinic-grant).

5. **Conquer Cancer Research Professorship (ovarian)** LOI ~late Sep is worth a **eligibility check for Stover** (senior/professor track). At $500k/5yr it is additive PI support, not a trial underwrite. Prefer ASCO official date **24 Sep 2026**. Source: [asco.org Research Professorship](https://www.asco.org/career-development/grants-awards/funding-opportunities/research-professorship).

**OCRA:** Best mid-size academic ovarian funder, but **nonprofit-only** and **2027 LOI window already closed** (25 Mar 2026). Park for early-2027. Source: [ocrahope.org apply](https://ocrahope.org/research/information-for-researchers/apply-for-a-grant).
