## What we can put in a non-confidential pack (sourced)

**Regulatory (public PRs / company site)**

- FDA **orphan drug designation** — DT2216 for T-cell lymphoma (company PR).
- FDA **Fast Track** — adult relapsed/refractory **PTCL and CTCL** (PR 5 Apr 2022).
- Texas company. CPRIT Seed, then **TXCO Product Development** (company cites **$14.4M**; CPRIT record DP210005: funds clinical trials of DT2216 in **T-cell lymphoma and SCLC**).
- IND and first-in-human already run: NCT04886622; Mahadevan et al., *J Hematol Oncol* 2025.

**Why CTCL is the heme story (published only)**

- Biology they already used in the orphan PR: mature T-cells drop BCL-XL after thymic selection; many T-cell lymphomas re-express it.
- He et al., *J Hematol Oncol* 2020 — MyLa CTCL xenograft: DT2216 monotherapy beat navitoclax (ABT-263), degraded BCL-XL, no BCL-2/MCL-1 compensation, less platelet toxicity, survival benefit in the model.
- Khan et al., *Nature Medicine* 2019 — MOLT-4 T-ALL: weekly DT2216 nearly complete growth inhibition vs daily navitoclax. Same degradation / no compensation pattern.

**Human data that is real, and that we must not mislabel as CTCL**

- Phase 1a (Mahadevan 2025): n=20 advanced **solid tumors**; RP2D 0.4 mg/kg IV BIW; complete BCL-XL degradation in PBMCs at RP2D; transient thrombocytopenia 45%; 1 DLT, resolved; best RECIST = SD 20%; authors say monotherapy lacked single-agent activity in solid tumors.
- Live company-sponsored combination study is **PROC**, NCT06964009, DFCI / Stover — weekly paclitaxel. That is the clinical operation, not CTCL.

**Do not put in a TAP inquiry (unpublished or over-claim)**

- DF83 / OVCAR3 complete-regression stories.
- “MD Anderson CTCL Phase 2 in 2026 / 2027” unless Josh produces a protocol number, PI, and IRB date. Our own July files disagree (2H 2026 vs H2 2027).
- Any implication that Phase 1a enrolled CTCL or showed CTCL responses. It did not.
- Clinical-hold language until Josh clears what is disclosable. August meetings: PROC study described as on **full clinical hold**, amendment at OPCRB, 3-month stability pull 18 Sep. If TAP diligence reaches the sites, they will hear a version of this. Decide the sentence now; do not improvise later.

## The argument that survives diligence

One paragraph, for the inquiry and slide 1:

DT2216 is a BCL-XL PROTAC with FDA orphan designation in T-cell lymphoma and Fast Track in relapsed/refractory PTCL and CTCL. Published CTCL and T-ALL xenografts show single-agent activity versus navitoclax with less platelet toxicity and no BCL-2/MCL-1 compensation — the opposite of the solid-tumor Phase 1a, where monotherapy was not active and the drug is now in combination. The ask is to fund the first CTCL (and/or PTCL) clinical cohort under the existing IND, not to rediscover the target.

That is the whole thesis. Everything else is support or a hole.
