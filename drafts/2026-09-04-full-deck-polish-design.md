# Dialectic Investor Presentation: Full-Deck Polish Design

Date: September 4, 2026

## Objective

Produce a separate, investor-ready revision of the 34-slide presentation. Preserve the subject and order of every slide, but make the full deck read and look as though one experienced company team prepared it at one time.

The revision must be accurate, restrained and easy to present. It must not contain recognizable AI phrasing, mechanical labels, slogan chains, decorative arrows, generic marketing language or repetitive three-part constructions.

## Recommendation

Use a full-system polish rather than a formatting-only pass.

The current presentation contains a strong rewritten core, especially the investment summary, ovarian scientific sequence and clinical translation slides. The surrounding legacy slides remain materially denser, use inconsistent visual conventions and sometimes present old milestones or broad claims without enough qualification. A formatting-only pass would preserve those weaknesses.

The recommended revision will:

1. Keep 34 slides and preserve the existing topic order.
2. Shorten repetitive prose while retaining the evidence needed to support each conclusion.
3. Apply one visual system to every slide.
4. Verify material claims against primary or controlling sources.
5. Distinguish published findings, submitted-manuscript data, company estimates and forward-looking plans.
6. Keep the current clinical thesis clear: DT2216 plus weekly paclitaxel in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

## Narrative

The deck should move through five questions in sequence:

1. What is the investment proposition?
2. Why can DT2216 address a biologically validated target that prior inhibitors could not dose adequately?
3. Why should paclitaxel create a useful combination in ovarian cancer?
4. What has been established in humans, and what remains to be shown?
5. Which clinical readouts will create or impair value?

Other indications and platform programs remain in the deck, but they should not compete with the ovarian program for attention. They provide evidence of platform breadth and additional development options.

## Visual System

Retain the Dialectic logo, teal color family and existing widescreen format.

- Use a consistent title bar, title position, footer and page-number treatment.
- Use conclusion-style titles on analytical slides.
- Use one restrained body typeface and one display weight.
- Use white or very light backgrounds for evidence slides and dark teal only for dividers or major conclusions.
- Use teal for the principal point, navy for supporting information, gray for context and red only for a genuine limitation or risk.
- Set normal body copy at 15 points or larger. Exceptions are limited to the legal disclaimer, source notes and labels embedded in original scientific figures. Sources may be 9 to 10 points.
- Give each slide one principal message. Reduce secondary text before reducing font size.
- Enlarge charts and manuscript panels until axes, legends and treatment labels can be read in presentation view.
- Avoid decorative title underlines, gratuitous icons, chevron chains and rounded-card grids that do not convey information.

## Accuracy Standard

Absolute certainty cannot be promised for forward-looking company information. The release standard is that no known factual statement remains unsupported or misleading.

Use this source hierarchy:

1. Controlling primary documents supplied by the company or investigators.
2. Peer-reviewed publications and registered clinical-trial records.
3. The July 2026 submitted Qin manuscript for confidential scientific findings.
4. Company presentation materials for management plans, estimates and unpublished programs.
5. Secondary sources only when a primary source is unavailable, with the limitation stated.

For every material scientific, clinical, pipeline, market, collaboration and IP statement:

- record the source;
- verify names, dates, units, doses, sample sizes and qualifiers;
- separate observations from interpretation;
- label submitted, unpublished and company-estimate information;
- remove or soften any claim that cannot be verified.
