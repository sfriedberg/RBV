## Slides 17–22

### Slide 17 — The therapeutic design

**Headline:** DT2216 plus paclitaxel targets complementary survival pathways

Use one restrained scientific diagram comparing the treatment approaches:

1. **BCL-XL inhibition alone**
   - Rapid apoptotic adaptation through compensatory pro-survival signaling, including MCL-1, limits single-agent activity in most solid tumors.
2. **Direct BCL-XL plus MCL-1 inhibition**
   - Synergistic apoptosis occurred in 88.6% of 132 tested cell lines, but direct dual inhibition is not tolerated in vivo because of on-target tissue toxicity.
3. **DT2216 plus paclitaxel**
   - Paclitaxel indirectly depletes MCL-1 and reduces HSP27-mediated adaptation.
   - DT2216 degrades BCL-XL while sparing sustained exposure in platelets.
   - The combination functionally co-targets both survival pathways without administering a direct MCL-1 inhibitor.

The slide should explain in conventional scientific language that paclitaxel-induced MCL-1 loss permits DT2216-mediated BCL-XL degradation to reproduce the antitumor effect of dual apoptotic targeting without administering a direct MCL-1 inhibitor.
