## What Larry already locked

The useful constraint is still the filter: **ADC, KRAS, or SERD**. Do not reopen Aurora A or PARP. The approach order that survived the independent pass is still **AstraZeneca, Amgen, AbbVie, then Lilly**.

The mistake to avoid is treating that list as a four-company roadshow. It is a sequence of one-mechanism meetings.

## Ideas worth doing

### 1. One mechanism per meeting, one asset named

Do not walk into AstraZeneca with Enhertu and camizestrant on the same slide. Pick **camizestrant** or **a single DXd ADC**, bring one indication, BCL-XL PD, and the platelet window. Same rule at Lilly: **Inluriyo first**, folate-receptor ADC later. Larry’s screen already implied this; the idea is to make it the only format we will take.

### 2. Amgen is the only “published combo” call — use it as the template

Lv et al. (2022) is still the only large-company paper that names DT2216 with a marketed KRAS drug. Ask Amgen for a **funded in-vivo repeat plus chronic platelet window**, not a tour of the pipeline. If they say no, take the same protocol to BMS/adagrasib and treat it as a class test. Do not start at Revolution Medicines.

### 3. AbbVie is a contrast meeting, not a collaboration fantasy

The idea is to put DT2216 (systemic, claimed platelet-sparing degrader) next to **ABBV-155** (tissue-targeted BCL-XL payload) and ask for the setting where a systemic degrader is the better tool — FRα-low, post-Elahere, or biomarker-agnostic. If that contrast is not allowed in the room, skip AbbVie. Highest value, highest chance they already own the answer.

### 4. Tie BD to the trial that already exists

The live asset is **DT2216 + weekly paclitaxel in PROC, DFCI / Stover**. Every first meeting should open with that protocol, not a hypothetical KRAS or SERD study. Combo ideas become “add-on cohorts or investigator-sponsored work after the Ph1b is moving,” not a new company-sponsored Phase 1 for each partner.

### 5. Use the September grant clock as a forcing function

CDMRP OCRP CTA LOI is **15 Sep 2026**. That is a better near-term use of Larry/Josh/Daohong time than a fourth company in the 10×3 table. Idea: one paragraph in the LOI that names **possible ADC or SERD combination work after the paclitaxel backbone is established** — without promising a partner. Gateway LOI (Nov) can carry the same sentence.

### 6. Menarini / Orserdu as the “small enough to answer”

If AZ and Lilly are slow, Menarini is the SERD that already sells. Approachable, thinner platform. Idea: a **single ESR1-mutant model package** and a 30-minute scientific call, not a term-sheet hunt. Useful as a rehearsal for the AZ camizestrant meeting.

### 7. What not to spend cycles on

- Building a fifth 10×3 table.
- Hengrui / Kelun / RemeGen until a Western rights path is named.
- Sanofi / amcenestrant (dead).
- Presenting DT2216 as an Elahere replacement.
- Mixing this note into a John Harkey IC package.
