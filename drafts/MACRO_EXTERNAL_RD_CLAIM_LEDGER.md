### Slide 6 (New — Slide A): External innovation supplies roughly two-thirds of Big Pharma product revenue

1. **Claim**: In 2025, 68% ($524B of $775B total; rounding of $340B + $184B) of Top-25 biopharma product revenue derived from externally sourced dealmaking: 44% ($340B) from M&A and 24% ($184B) from alliances and joint ventures, while organic internally discovered revenue accounted for 32% ($252B), down from 39% ($162B of $412B total) in 2015.
   - **Status**: VERIFIED.
   - **Source**: EY *2026 Biotech Beyond Borders Report* (June 2026), Portfolio section & databook table "Top 25 biopharma product revenue (US$b), 2015, 2025, 2030E" (citing EY analysis, Evaluate Pharma, Capital IQ):
     - 2015 total $412B: Organic $162B (39%), M&A $181B (44%), Alliances/JVs $70B (17%) -> 61% external.
     - 2025 total $775B: Organic $252B (32%), M&A $340B (44%), Alliances/JVs $184B (24%) -> 68% external ($524B).
     - 2015–2025 CAGRs: Alliances/JVs 10.2%, M&A 6.5%, Organic 4.5%.

2. **Claim**: Emerging biopharma (EBP; defined by IQVIA as companies with <$200M estimated annual R&D spend and <$500M annual pharmaceutical revenue) accounts for 65%–68% of global clinical trial starts and active R&D pipeline (up from 33% of the active pipeline in 2002 to 67% of the active pipeline in 2022, 65% of trial starts in 2023, and 68% of all clinical trial starts in 2025), whereas large pharma accounts for 26% of trial starts.
   - **Status**: VERIFIED.
   - **Sources**:
     - IQVIA *Global Trends in R&D 2023* (Feb 2023): EBP definition (<$200M R&D spend, <$500M sales); EBPs accounted for 67% of the active R&D pipeline in 2022, up from 33% in 2002 (and 51% in 2017).
     - IQVIA *Global Trends in R&D 2025* (Mar 2025, Exhibit 2 & Clinical Trial Activity): EBPs accounted for 65% of trial starts in 2023 (63% in 2024; 65% of Phase I starts in 2024), while large pharma (> $10B sales) accounted for 26% of trial starts across phases in 2024 (down from 47% in 2015).
     - IQVIA *Global R&D Trends 2026* (Mar 2026) & IQVIA May 2026 clinical trial analysis: EBP sponsors accounted for 68% of all industry-sponsored clinical trial starts in 2025 (up from 56% in 2019).

3. **Claim**: Emerging biopharma companies originated 61%–85% of novel active substances (NAS) launched in 2024–2025 (85% of U.S. NAS launches in 2024 [41 of 48]; 61% of the 79 global NAS launches in 2025).
   - **Status**: VERIFIED (with geographic basis stated explicitly so U.S. vs. global denominators are not conflated).
   - **Sources**: IQVIA *Global Trends in R&D 2025* (Exhibit 38: 41 of 48 [85%] U.S. NAS launches in 2024 originated by EBPs; 63% both EBP-originated and EBP-launched); IQVIA *Global R&D Trends 2026* (79 global NAS launches in 2025, 61% originated by EBPs).

4. **Claim**: Patent cliff (loss of exclusivity, LOE) and R&D cycle-time mismatch: ~$220B (IQVIA 2025–2029 developed-market branded LOE impact) to $300B+ (Evaluate global sales at risk through 2030) in branded sales face loss of exclusivity, including major oncology franchises (Xtandi and Ibrance in 2027; Keytruda and Opdivo in 2028; Darzalex in 2029), outpacing 10 to 14 year internal R&D timelines.
   - **Status**: VERIFIED.
   - **Sources**:
     - IQVIA *The Global Use of Medicines 2025: Outlook to 2029* (May 2025) & *Unlocking the Strategic Value of Established Brands* (2026, Fig. 2): $220.1B (~$220B) in branded sales impacted by LOE over 2025–2029 (~4x the $63.8B impact in 2020–2024).
     - Evaluate Pharma *World Preview* / patent-cliff analyses: $300B+ in branded prescription sales at risk through 2028–2030 (peaking near $99B–$104B in 2028).
     - IQVIA *Global Trends in R&D 2025* (Exhibit 40: >75% of 2024 U.S. NAS launches occurred >10 years after first patent/trial, median 14 years) and *Global R&D Trends 2026* (median clinical R&D program duration 10.0 years in 2025).

5. **Claim**: Structural division of R&D labor: focused biotechs specialize in novel modalities (such as VHL-recruiting protein degraders and precision combinations) to absorb early target-and-modality risk, while Big Pharma deploys balance-sheet scale at registrational Phase 2/3 and global commercialization.
   - **Status**: INTERPRETATION grounded in EY 2026 and IQVIA 2025/2026 phase-distribution data.

### Slide 7 (New — Slide B): Phase 1b/2 clinical proof is a primary pharma partnership and acquisition seam

1. **Claim**: Recent platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (PROC) acquisitions demonstrate multi-billion-dollar pharma demand at both early clinical proof-of-concept and post-approval stages:
   - **Genmab / ProfoundBio ($1.8B, announced April 2024; closed May 2024)**: All-cash acquisition of Rina-S (rinatabart sesutecan, FRα-directed ADC) following Phase 1/2 ovarian cancer clinical readout (`NCT05579366`, RAINFOL-01) prior to Phase 3 initiation or completion.
   - **AbbVie / ImmunoGen ($10.1B, announced November 2023; closed February 2024)**: Acquisition of Elahere (mirvetuximab soravtansine-gynx, FRα-directed DM4 ADC), approved in FRα-high PROC (~36% of screened PROC patients eligible; 31.7% ORR in single-arm SORAYA accelerated approval and 41.9% ORR in Phase 3 MIRASOL full approval; boxed warning for ocular toxicity).
   - **Status**: VERIFIED.
   - **Sources**: AbbVie/ImmunoGen press releases (Nov 30, 2023; Feb 12, 2024; FDA label Mar 2024); Matulonis et al., *J Clin Oncol* 2023 (SORAYA 31.7% ORR); St. Laurent & Liu, *J Clin Oncol* 2023 (~36% FRα-high eligibility); Genmab/ProfoundBio press releases (Apr 3, 2024; May 21, 2024); company presentation extract Slides 27–28.

2. **Claim**: Dialectic's established foundation and active inflection at this seam:
   - *Established*: First-in-human Phase 1a completed (`0.40 mg/kg` IV twice-weekly RP2D; rapid, sustained peripheral-leukocyte BCL-XL degradation; transient thrombocytopenia in 9/20 patients with 1 grade 4 DLT resolving within 48h; Mahadevan et al., *JHO* 2025) + 7 of 8 HGSOC PDX models with ex-vivo synergy and durable flank and disseminated DF83 PDX regressions (`Qin et al.`, confidential submitted manuscript, July 2026).
   - *Active clinical inflection*: Recruiting Dana-Farber-led Phase 1b trial (`NCT06964009`, `DT2216 + weekly paclitaxel`, BOIN design, estimated enrollment 30, primary registered outcome: combination MTD) in biomarker-unrestricted PROC (not limited to the ~36% FRα-high population).
   - *Combination & BD optionality*: Preclinical combination sensitization across mitotic-arrest agents and free microtubule ADC payloads (DM4, MMAE, MMAF in OVCAR8 in vitro; `Qin et al.`, July 2026), `KRAS G12C` inhibition (`Lv et al.`, *JHO* 2022), and unpublished `ER+` breast `SERD` models (`Li et al.`), supporting disciplined BD structures (co-sponsored clinical combination study or gated translational/preclinical collaboration under CDA; `Progress and Capital Plan`, July 7, 2026, Slide 7).
   - **Status**: VERIFIED (with confidential-manuscript and in-vitro qualifiers).
